title: Automod
description: Filtered terms, regex rules, invite/link blocking, and heat-based escalation.
---

The `filter` module (🚫) is Lumi's automod rule engine: filtered terms matched with
Aho-Corasick, regex rules, invite/link blocking with allowlists, mention-spam and caps limits,
and a decaying per-member heat score for escalation. It has no slash commands of its own —
everything is configured through the dashboard or `/config`.

## Terms and patterns

- **Filtered Terms** (`terms`) — words/phrases to block, one per line, matched with
  Aho-Corasick.
- **Regex Rules** (`regex_rules`) — case-insensitive regular expressions, max 256 characters
  each, one per line. Invalid patterns are skipped; patterns that would backtrack
  catastrophically are rejected when saved.

## Invites and links

- **Block Invites** (`block_invites`) deletes messages containing Discord invites, except codes
  on the **Allowed Invite Codes** allowlist (e.g. your own server's).
- **Block Links** (`block_links`) deletes messages containing links, except domains on the
  **Allowed Link Domains** allowlist (subdomains included).

## Spam limits

- **Max Mentions** (`max_mentions`) — user+role mentions per message before the message is
  removed. `0` disables the rule.
- **Max Caps %** (`max_caps_percent`) — delete messages whose letters are more than this percent
  uppercase, only once a message is at least **Caps Min Length** (`caps_min_length`) characters
  long.

## Punishment

- **Exempt Roles** (`exempt_roles`) bypass every filter rule. Members with Manage Messages are
  always exempt.
- **Timeout** (`timeout_minutes`) times out the author on a filter hit; `0` disables the
  punishment.
- **Warning Message** (`warn_message`) is a transient reply after a deletion, with `{user}` and
  `{reason}` placeholders. Empty disables it.
- **Log Channel** (`log_channel_id`) receives filter hit logs.

## Heat scoring

When **Heat System** (`heat_enabled`) is on, each member accrues a decaying heat score:
per-message, per-mention, per-duplicate-message, per-filter-hit, per-attachment, per-emoji, and
per-link weights all add heat (`heat_per_*`), a webhook multiplier
(`heat_webhook_multiplier`) scales message/link heat for webhook-sent messages, and heat bleeds
off at a configurable rate (`heat_decay_per_minute`).

Three thresholds escalate independently: **Warn Threshold** (`heat_warn`) sends a slow-down
warning, **Timeout Threshold** (`heat_timeout`) times the member out for `heat_timeout_minutes`,
and **Quarantine Threshold** (`heat_quarantine`) quarantines them. `0` disables any of the three.

**Escalating Timeouts** (`heat_multiplier_enabled`) geometrically increases the timeout duration
for members who keep re-tripping heat after their previous timeout expires, by
`heat_multiplier_base` each repeat (e.g. base `2` → 1x, 1x, 2x, 4x, 8x the base duration).

**Heat panic mode**: if `heat_panic_raider_count` distinct members trip Timeout or Quarantine
within `heat_panic_window_seconds`, panic mode activates and instantly actions any flagged
member's next message — a defense against coordinated raid bursts, not just individual spam.

## Auto lockdown

Independent of heat: if total non-exempt mentions guild-wide exceed
`lockdown_mention_threshold` within `lockdown_window_seconds`, the server enters an automatic
lockdown for `lockdown_duration_minutes` before auto-unlocking. `0` disables it. This is the
automatic counterpart to `mod`'s manual `/lockdown` command.

All config fields are also listed, generated from the module's manifest, wherever the dashboard
renders `filter`'s settings form.
